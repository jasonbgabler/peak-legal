Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

Peak stores only what you choose to log: your username and email (for your account), your meals, activities, mood check-ins, goals, weights, height, and lifestyle selections.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in your private, per-user account on Supabase (our database and authentication provider) and is protected by row-level security, so only you can read or write it. Passwords are handled by Supabase authentication and are never stored by Peak in plain text.

How Claude is used

When you describe a meal or submit a mind check-in, that text is sent through our secure backend to Anthropic's Claude API to identify foods or generate a suggestion. It is not used to train models and is not stored by Peak beyond producing your result. Exact nutrition figures come from the USDA FoodData Central public database.

Crash reports and app analytics

So we can fix bugs and understand which parts of Peak people actually use, we rely on two more service providers: Sentry for crash and error reporting, and PostHog for product analytics. They receive technical information — your device type and app version, the details of a crash when one happens, and coarse signals such as which screen was opened or that a meal was logged — together with your Peak account identifier, so we can tell whether a problem hit one person or everyone.

They never receive the content of what you log. No meal descriptions, no mood or body-check notes, no goals, no weights, no free-form health text of any kind is sent to either service.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party ads. We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking, and none of the services above track you across other apps or websites. We do not share your data except with the service providers named above that make the app work.

Your control

You can export all of your data as a JSON file at any time from Profile → Data & privacy. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the same place; deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Health disclaimer

Peak is a wellness and self-tracking tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. Calorie targets, burn estimates, nutrition figures, and mind check-in suggestions are general wellness guidance, not diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet, exercise, or health routine.
