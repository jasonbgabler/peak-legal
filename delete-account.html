Your data

Delete your PEAK account

You can permanently delete your PEAK account and everything stored with it at any time. Deleting can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PEAK and tap your Profile button in the top right.
  2. Open Data & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete your account and confirm.

Want a copy first? Export your data is in the same section and saves everything as one file.

By email

If you can't open the app, email jasonbgabler@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done.

What's deleted

Your profile, every meal, workout, check-in, note, plan, goal and saved item, your connections and what you shared with them, and your sign-in. Shared reference data that was never linked to you (such as cached food lookups) isn't personal data and stays, and anything PEAK kept only on your phone is removed when you delete the app. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
