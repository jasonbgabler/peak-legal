Common questions

How do I log a meal or a workout?

The fastest way is to tell your coach: on the Coach tab, type or say what you ate or how you moved ("had oatmeal and coffee", "ran 3 miles in 30 minutes") and it's logged, with Undo and Change under it. Or use the box at the top of the Diet or Fitness tab. On Diet you can also take a photo, scan a barcode or pick from your kitchen.

How do I log something for an earlier day?

Say the day to your coach ("I forgot to log a hike yesterday"), or include it in what you type in the Diet or Fitness box. The log screens also have Which day? (Today, Yesterday, Earlier). To browse a past day, open Log history on that tab and tap the calendar icon.

How do I change or delete something I logged?

Open Log history on the Diet, Fitness or Wellness tab, pick the day, and tap the entry to change it, its time or its day, or to delete it. Right after the coach logs something, Undo and Change sit under it.

My plan logged a meal or workout I didn't do.

Planned items log themselves half an hour after their time. Open Log history, find the item marked "From your plan" and tap Skipped it?, or tap the item to change what happened. To stop plans logging themselves, turn off Log my plan for me at the foot of Plan ahead (the Plan tile on Diet or Fitness).

How do I change my calorie, macro, burn or step targets?

Open the menu (three lines, top left), tap your name for Profile, then Daily targets. Your goal, goal weight and reach-by date are under Goal.

How do I connect Apple Health or my calendar?

Open the menu and tap Integrations. Apple Health brings in workouts, steps, sleep and resting heart rate. Calendar sync puts planned meals and workouts on your phone's calendar.

I forgot my password.

On the Log in screen tap Forgot password?. PEAK emails you a link that opens a page on usepeakapp.com where you choose a new one. Each link works once, for one hour.

Sign in with Apple or Google opened a new, empty account.

Log in with your email and password instead, then go to Profile › Username and sign-in › Sign-in methods and tap Connect beside Apple or Google. After that, either one opens your real account.

How do I invite a partner, join a community or connect with a coach?

Open the menu and tap Sharing. Share an invite link, or tap Have a code? to join with a code someone sent you. You choose what each connection sees, and you can pause or end sharing at any time.

How do I tell the coach about allergies or foods I avoid?

On the Coach tab tap Coach's memory, then About you. Food suggestions, menu picks and meal plans leave those foods out, and a scanned label flags them. PEAK never tells you a food is safe, so always check the package or ask the restaurant.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Profile › Notifications. Each reminder has its own switch and time.

What does PEAK use my location for?

Recording a route you start, and, only when you ask your coach, today's weather or places nearby. Profile › Location shows what's allowed and has a switch for travel tips. PEAK's servers never store your location.

Can I hide calorie numbers?

Yes. Profile › Appearance › Calories on the main screens › Hide.

Why do calorie numbers look approximate?

PEAK uses AI to identify the foods you describe or photograph, then takes their nutrition from USDA FoodData Central and, for packaged foods, the product label. Portions are estimates you can adjust before or after logging. Treat the figures as good general guidance, not lab measurements.

Is PEAK medical advice?

No. PEAK is a wellness tool for self-tracking and general guidance. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk with a qualified professional about medical or mental-health concerns. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services (in the US, call or text 988).
