Common questions

Why do calorie numbers look approximate? Peak pairs Claude's food identification with USDA FoodData Central nutrition data — figures are good estimates for general guidance, not lab measurements.

Is Peak medical advice? No. Peak is a wellness tracker. Always consult a qualified professional for medical or mental-health concerns.
