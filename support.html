Common questions

How do I log something?

Tell your coach on the Coach tab, or use the box at the top of Diet or Fitness. Say the day ("yesterday…") to log an earlier one.

How do I fix or delete an entry?

Open Log history on that tab, pick the day and tap the entry.

My plan logged something I didn't do.

Open Log history and tap Skipped it? on that item. To stop plans logging themselves, turn off Log my plan for me in Plan ahead.

How do I change my targets?

Menu (top left) › your name › Daily targets.

How do I connect Apple Health or my calendar?

Menu › Integrations.

I forgot my password.

On Log in, tap Forgot password? and follow the emailed link.

Apple or Google sign-in opened an empty account.

Log in with your email, then Profile › Username and sign-in › Sign-in methods › Connect.

How do I share with a partner?

Menu › Sharing. Invite with a link, or tap Have a code? to join.

Is PEAK medical advice?

No. PEAK is a wellness tool. For medical concerns, see a qualified professional. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988.

Your data

Export or delete everything from the menu › your name › Data & privacy. More on deleting your account.
