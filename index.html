Questions

Is PEAK free?

Yes, during early access. If paid features come later, we'll say what's included first.

When can I get it?

PEAK is in early access on iPhone. Email us for an invite. Android is on the roadmap.

Where does the advice come from?

From your own logs and goals, held to standards built on 30 named sources and reviewed by a dietitian, a strength coach and a mental-health professional. Answers are written by AI, so treat them as general guidance.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. It reads workouts, steps, sleep and resting heart rate, and saves your mindful minutes. Optional, and you can disconnect anytime.

How accurate is the nutrition?

Foods come from USDA data and, for packaged foods, the label itself. Adjust any portion before or after you log.

Is this medical advice?

No. PEAK is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Talk with a qualified professional about medical questions.
