Three tabs, one app

Diet, fitness and wellness, finally talking to each other.

Diet

Know what's on your plate.

Real nutrition from the words you'd use anyway.

  • Calories, protein, carbs, fat and eight vitamins and minerals per meal
  • Every meal shows where it lands: how much of today is left
  • A pantry of the foods you keep, saved recipes, and a shopping list
  • Meal plans built for your target, or imported from a plan you already follow

Fitness

Every kind of movement counts.

From a walk with the dog to a heavy lift day.

  • Workouts and steps from Apple Health, synced in on their own
  • Burn worked out from your weight, height and real pace
  • Bodyweight circuits, GPS routes for walks, runs, hikes and rides, and interval timers
  • A daily lap that fills toward your burn or step goal

Wellness

How you feel is data too.

The part most trackers leave out.

  • A one-touch check-in on a mountain sunrise that rises with your mood
  • One line of gratitude a day, typed or spoken
  • Guided breathing, short meditations and wind-down practices
  • A daily note of guidance written from your own week

Your personal coach

One coach for your whole day.

Part trainer, part nutritionist, part wellness coach. Ask a question or just tell it what you did: it reads your day and your goals, answers from whichever angle the question needs, and logs what you mention.

  • Logs as you talk. "Had oatmeal and coffee" becomes a breakfast entry, with Undo.
  • Answers with your numbers. What's left today, what your week looks like.
  • Remembers what matters. Tell it you're vegetarian once.

Patterns, not just totals

It finds what you'd never spot.

PEAK compares your own days against each other — mood against movement, sleep, steps and when you eat — and says what it finds in a sentence. Then you can test it.

  • Patterns in plain words, drawn only from your logs.
  • Two-week experiments that score whether a change actually helped, and say so when it didn't.
  • Cited habits from a library where every card names its source.

Questions

Good to know

Is PEAK free?

Yes, PEAK is free during early access. If paid features arrive later, we'll say what's included before anything changes.

When can I get it?

PEAK is in early access on iPhone now and coming soon to the App Store. Email us for an invite and we'll send one.

Is there an Android version?

PEAK is iPhone-first. Android is on the roadmap.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Connect Apple Health and PEAK reads your workouts, steps, sleep and resting heart rate, so you don't have to log them twice. It's optional, and you can disconnect any time.

How accurate is the nutrition?

PEAK identifies the foods you describe and takes their nutrition from USDA FoodData Central, including manufacturer label data for packaged and store-brand foods. You can adjust any portion before you log it.

Is this medical advice?

No. PEAK is a wellness tool for self-tracking and general guidance. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk with a qualified professional about medical questions.

Can I take my data with me, or delete it?

Yes. Export everything from Profile → Data & privacy, or delete your account and all of its data from the same place. More on deleting your account.
