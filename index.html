It knows your day, and it knows you.

  • Answers from your numbers. What you've eaten, how you've moved, how you slept and what's planned. One clear recommendation, with the figures behind it under Why.
  • Remembers what matters. Tell it once about allergies, foods you avoid, your equipment or a sore knee, and every suggestion works around them.
  • Knows where you are, when you ask. Real places nearby with a link to Maps, a day out planned around your goals, and hotel-room workouts when you're traveling.
  • Talk instead of type. The mic listens until you're done, and the coach can read its answers aloud.

A coach you can check

Built on named sources. Reviewed by professionals.

Cited

The coach's nutrition, fitness and wellness standards rest on 30 named sources, from national guidelines to peer-reviewed research. An answer can show its source under Why.

Reviewed

A registered dietitian, a certified strength and conditioning coach and a licensed mental-health professional reviewed those standards and the coach's safety replies.

Careful

Allergies are hard limits. It won't suggest supplement doses, train through pain or go under a safe calorie floor, and it points you to a professional when a question needs one.

Diet, fitness and wellness, finally talking to each other.

Five tabs. The coach up front, one tab for each area, and Progress for where you stand.

Diet

Know what's on your plate.

Real nutrition from the words you'd use anyway.

  • Calories, protein, carbs, fat and eight vitamins and minerals per meal
  • Every meal shows where it lands: how much of today is left
  • Packaged foods read from the label: ingredients, allergens and additives
  • Your usuals, the meals you keep logging, one tap each
  • A kitchen you can fill from a receipt photo, saved recipes, and a grocery list to share with a partner
  • Meal plans built for your target or from your kitchen, or imported from a plan you follow

Fitness

Every kind of movement counts.

From a walk with the dog to a heavy lift day.

  • Workouts and steps from Apple Health, synced in on their own
  • Burn worked out from your weight and real pace
  • Follow-along circuits and interval timers with spoken cues
  • GPS routes for walks, runs, hikes and rides that keep tracking with the screen off
  • A daily lap that fills toward your burn or step goal

Wellness

How you feel is data too.

The part most trackers leave out.

  • A one-touch check-in on a mountain sunrise that rises with your mood
  • One line of gratitude a day, typed or spoken
  • Guided breathing, meditations and wind-down practices, with a voice guide and mindful minutes saved to Apple Health
  • Reflect: two minutes at night, one plan for tomorrow
  • A daily note of guidance written from your own week, and a library of cited habits

Plan once, follow all week

Plan your week. Let it log itself.

Let PEAK fill a week of meals and workouts around your targets, build it from what's in your kitchen, import a plan you already follow, or ask your coach for a 4-week program. When a planned item's time passes, it's logged for you.

  • Didn't happen? Skip it before, or tap Skipped it or Changed it after. Or ask your coach to move, swap or drop anything in the next three weeks.
  • On your calendar. Planned meals and workouts sync to the calendar on your phone, with a reminder.
  • Weather heads-up. A run planned for a rainy afternoon comes with an indoor swap.
  • Straight to the store. One tap sends the week's ingredients to your grocery list, minus what's in your kitchen.

Questions

Good to know

Is PEAK free?

Yes, PEAK is free during early access. If paid features arrive later, we'll say what's included before anything changes.

When can I get it?

PEAK is in early access on iPhone now and coming soon to the App Store. Email us for an invite and we'll send one.

Is there an Android version?

PEAK is iPhone-first. Android is on the roadmap.

Can the coach log things for me?

Yes. Tell it what you ate or how you moved, today or an earlier day, and it logs it with Undo and Change underneath. If it can't tell what you meant, it asks one question first.

Where does the coach's advice come from?

From your own logs, goals and plans, held to written standards for nutrition, fitness and wellness. Those standards rest on 30 named sources and were reviewed by a registered dietitian, a certified strength and conditioning coach and a licensed mental-health professional. The answers themselves are written by AI, so treat them as general guidance.

Does it know about my allergies?

Tell your coach once and its food suggestions, menu picks and meal plans leave those foods out. When you scan a packaged food, PEAK flags any of your allergens the label lists. It never tells you a food is safe, so always check the package or ask the restaurant.

How do I sign in?

With Apple, with Google, or with an email and password. Face ID can unlock the app after that.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Connect Apple Health from the menu under Integrations and PEAK reads your workouts, steps, sleep and resting heart rate, so you don't have to log them twice, and saves the mindful minutes from your practices. Planned meals and workouts can also go on your phone's calendar. It's optional, and you can disconnect any time.

How accurate is the nutrition?

PEAK identifies the foods you describe and takes their nutrition from USDA FoodData Central, including manufacturer label data for packaged and store-brand foods. You can adjust any portion before you log it.

Is this medical advice?

No. PEAK is a wellness tool for self-tracking and general guidance. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk with a qualified professional about medical questions.

Can I take my data with me, or delete it?

Yes. Export everything from Profile → Data & privacy, or delete your account and all of its data from the same place. More on deleting your account.
